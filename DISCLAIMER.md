# Disclaimer

`athletevalue` produces statistical estimates for research and education.

- Estimates are not reports of what any athlete is paid. The "roster market value"
  it prints is an allocation of a published conference-tier average budget by role
  and rating. It contains no information about any player's contract and is labelled
  a scenario. Treating it, or a surplus derived from it, as a statement about a named
  person's compensation misreads the output.
- Program value is an association between a school's wins and the revenue it
  attributes to men's basketball in federal EADA filings. It is not a causal estimate
  and omits conference, donor and brand revenue; the direction of the net bias is
  unknown.
- The team table's "position among paid teammates" compares medians within one
  roster. It is a description of two model quantities, not an assessment of a player.
- Intervals reflect the uncertainty the models can measure, not every way they can
  be wrong. The replacement level is a choice that scales every wins and dollar
  figure; the summary shows the alternatives.
- Player names come from public NCAA box scores. The project is not affiliated with
  or endorsed by the NCAA, any conference, school, collective or data provider.
- Nothing here is financial, legal or employment advice. Corrections and removal
  requests: see PRIVACY.md.
